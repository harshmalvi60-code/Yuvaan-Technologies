Dear Valued Clients and Supporters,

I am thrilled to share with you the incredible journey we have embarked on since the inception of our brand. From the very beginning, our mission has been to revolutionize the tech industry by offering a comprehensive suite of services tailored to meet your digital marketing and branding needs.

As the founder of this company, I have witnessed firsthand the challenges faced by businesses in the ever-evolving digital landscape. Through my own experiences with running a clothing line and multiple e-commerce stores, I recognized the crucial role effective marketing plays in achieving success.

With that vision in mind, we have built a team of dedicated professionals who are passionate about delivering exceptional results. Throughout this journey, our success has been defined by your success. We take immense pride in the positive feedback we have received from our valued clients, and we are grateful for the trust you have placed in us.

I invite you to join us as we continue to push boundaries, redefine standards, and make a lasting impact in the digital marketing realm. Together, let us create remarkable experiences and unlock the full potential of your brand.

Thank you for being a part of our journey.

Our Story

From a small studio
to a full growth partner.

2016

The beginning

  • Swarna Mishra founds the company, envisioning a service business that offers digital marketing, social media management, website development, branding solutions, package design, and video production.
  • Early days focused on market research — identifying the real needs and demands of growing brands.
2017
—18

First clients, first wins

  • Launched services with a focus on website development and digital marketing.
  • Expanded to social media management and branding solutions as the business gained traction.
  • Invested in a skilled team of professionals to handle a growing client base.
2019

Design joins the stack

  • Added package design to the range of services, recognizing the role of visual appeal in commerce.
  • Continued evolving alongside industry trends and incorporating new technologies.
2020

Going fully digital

  • Significant growth as businesses sought digital solutions during the pandemic.
  • Adapted to remote work and built innovative digital tools to serve clients seamlessly.
  • Expanded the clientele across industries globally — startups to established enterprises.
2021
—22

Becoming a brand house

  • Strengthened our position as a comprehensive branding solutions partner.
  • Launched video production to create engaging visual stories for clients.
  • Built tailored strategies that maximize online presence across channels.
2023

New verticals, same mission

  • Expanded into burgeoning niches — institutes, schools, restaurants — while keeping leadership in digital.
  • Continued to assist businesses with a comprehensive range of services.
2024

Storytelling at scale

  • Maintaining our position as a leader in the digital marketing industry.
  • Helping brands achieve their goals through services that include compelling visual narratives.

Yuvaan Technologies is a creative digital agency and a software-solutions partner for businesses. Our main services include — and are not limited to — web design, digital marketing, and branding.
